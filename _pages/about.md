I am now a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in [Chemical Engineering at UMass Amherst](https://www.umass.edu/engineering/chemical-engineering). I am under the supervision of [Prof. Peng Bai](https://people.umass.edu/baigroup/). Before coming to UMass, I conducted research work on pursusing my Master of Science in [Chemical Enginnering at Oregon State University](https://engineering.oregonstate.edu/academics/programs/chemical-engineering), under the supervision of [Prof. Cory M. Simon](https://simonensemble.github.io/).

My research interest includes levering machine learning and bayesian optimization to efficiently guide the search for porous material with target properties.
